# Website guidance

## Navigation preference
The owner prefers a full product website with dedicated Features, Pricing, Support, Guides and FAQ pages. Preserve the established visual identity. Header and footer navigation must use real page URLs, including /pricing rather than /#pricing. Keep footer links grouped by Product, Learn, Support and Legal. Maintain a usable mobile navigation menu. Existing topic-specific FAQ pages and guides are the source of truth; avoid duplicating them as thin pages.
