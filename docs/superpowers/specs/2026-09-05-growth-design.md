# ClearDisk growth and measurement

Authorized scope: add download tracking with a graph, research long-tail search demand, publish useful articles and sitemap, recommend marketing. Keep test-only payment, current brand, cleardisk.app and Talivia pause.

Use a Cloudflare SQLite Durable Object bound to the existing Worker for daily aggregate download requests, split by normalized referral channel. Intercept successful full GET requests to the existing DMG route; exclude HEAD/ranges/known bots, do not store IPs, identifiers or raw referrers. This measures requests, not completed transfers, unique people or installs. Tracking failures must never block downloads. Keep 366 days and disclose collection. A secret-protected, noindex owner dashboard shows 30 days, 7/30-day totals, channel breakdown and a separate live GitHub release download count. Basic authentication over HTTPS avoids adding login/session machinery; store a random password in Wrangler secrets and an ignored local credentials file. No public write API; no tracking cookies.

Publish 12 distinct source-checked articles in two isolated content files, integrated with existing five guides, SSR, unique canonical metadata, Article/Breadcrumb schema and sitemap. Topics must resolve independent user problems, with concrete steps, limits and primary references. No invented volume/KD, rankings, testimonials or capability claims. Research reports distinguish directional demand from unavailable volume metrics. Group the hub for navigation. Marketing plan prioritizes product demos, helpful community participation and creator outreach; do not post/send messages or buy ads.
