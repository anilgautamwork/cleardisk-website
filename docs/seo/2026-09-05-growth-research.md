# ClearDisk search research and 30-day growth plan

Research date: 2026-09-05. Scope: English-language Mac storage searches, global audience with US commercial priority as a planning assumption. This is directional research, not a search-volume forecast. No authenticated keyword-volume provider, Search Console property or paid campaign data was available for this research. Every monthly-volume cell in [growth-keywords.csv](growth-keywords.csv) is therefore `unverified`; no keyword difficulty or ranking estimates are supplied.

## What the search evidence establishes

Public web searches showed recurring problem themes and a mixture of official support pages, publishers, cleaner vendors, videos and user discussions. That establishes that these topics have visible search results and a useful answer format. It does **not** establish how many people search each phrase, that the result order matches a US Google SERP, or that ClearDisk can rank for it. No autocomplete, People Also Ask, Google Trends or authenticated keyword-volume data was collected. Related keyword phrasings in the CSV are editorial hypotheses grouped under observed topics, not claimed exact-match searches from a volume tool.

Queries used included `mac system data 100GB keeps growing storage full after deleting files`, `find large files mac delete iphone backups purgeable space`, `icloud drive taking up space mac move photos library external drive`, `find node_modules folders mac disk space`, `docker raw taking up space mac`, `delete iphone backups mac`, and `icloud drive taking up space mac remove download`. Targeted primary-documentation queries also covered Xcode Derived Data, simulator runtime removal, browser cache clearing and Photoshop scratch disks.

## Priorities and page mapping

The CSV maps 45 candidate phrases to 17 guide URLs: five existing guides and twelve additions. Each URL owns an independent user problem. Synonyms share that URL, preventing duplicate “100GB,” “200GB,” MacBook Air and MacBook Pro pages with the same answer.

Intent and product fit are separate judgments. `intent_strength` is high for an immediate practical problem, medium for explanation; it does not mean purchase intent. `product_fit` is high when local file discovery directly helps, medium when the owning app is the main control, and low when the issue primarily concerns cloud behavior or application configuration. A useful browser or Photos answer can have lower product fit without being lower quality. `priority` is an editorial sequence, not a prediction of rank or search volume.

Lead promotion with System Data explanation/cleanup, large-file discovery and developer storage inspection. These connect naturally to a free local scan. Keep iCloud, purgeable storage, browser and creative-workflow pages helpful on their own; their native controls may finish the task without a download. Do not imply ClearDisk removes every byte of System Data, manages Docker volumes, configures Photoshop or repairs cloud sync.

## Search implementation guidance

Publish substantive, sourced answers with clear ownership and dates. Explain what must be kept, what can be recreated and how the reader checks the result. Do not inflate claims or add dates without meaningful review. Google emphasizes original value, completeness and an audience-focused purpose; a prescribed word count is not a ranking strategy. [Google people-first content guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/creating-helpful-content).

Use one canonical HTTPS URL per guide, matching the URL in internal links and the sitemap. Keep each title and description specific to the actual problem. The hub should link to all 17 pages in understandable groups; related links should help the next decision. Canonicalization signals influence Google's selection but do not force it. [Google canonical guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/consolidate-duplicate-urls).

Serve full article text, metadata and links in the initial HTML. Include public canonical pages in an absolute-URL sitemap; exclude the private analytics view, preview-only routes and test-checkout utility pages. Only update lastmod for real content changes. After release, verify the sitemap in Search Console if property access is available; submission helps discovery and does not guarantee indexing. [Google sitemap guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/sitemaps/build-sitemap).

Article and Breadcrumb structured data must describe the visible content accurately. Dates and organization authorship must be real. Validate generated markup; do not invent ratings, reviews, personal author credentials or other unsupported attributes. Structured data alone does not guarantee enhanced appearance. [Google Article guidance](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/appearance/structured-data/article).

The proposed dashboard counts successful full DMG GET requests, not unique visitors, completed downloads, installations or activated users. Retries and unidentified automation may contribute; known-bot exclusions are imperfect. Coarse referrer/campaign buckets do not recover first-touch attribution when a visitor moves between pages without attribution being preserved. A separate live GitHub release count is another series, not a number to add blindly to the Worker series. Do not calculate download conversion rates without a compatible page-view denominator. Maintain no cookies, raw referrers, IP addresses or user identifiers for this aggregate measurement.

At day 30, prefer a modest decision supported by evidence: improve a confusing download flow, clarify a high-impression article, or continue a relevant demo channel. There is no evidence yet for a paid-acquisition budget, a target cost per install or a forecast of license sales.

## Content verification notes

The six developer/application guides each contain roughly 540 words including headings and summary, five concrete sections, version qualifications and primary-source links. Shell examples only list or measure directories and Docker objects; the guide describes npm ci separately as a modifying reinstall operation. No blanket recursive deletion command is supplied. Related links target the existing five guides or the six guides in the same module.

The instructions are source-checked, not the result of running destructive cleanup on a test matrix of app versions. In particular, Apple’s current documents show more than one Components removal UI; the guide describes both and defers to the installed version. Xcode 27’s new Derived Data action is confirmed by Apple’s WWDC26 material, without claiming availability in earlier releases. Adobe’s 100GB suggestion is expressly attributed and scoped to its current Photoshop troubleshooting article. Recheck vendor UI and support documents during future app/macOS releases.
