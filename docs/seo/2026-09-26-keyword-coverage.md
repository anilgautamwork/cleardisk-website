## Search intent and editorial decisions

- Searches for CleanMyMac or AppCleaner can be navigational: someone may want that maker's app. The pages identify the makers, provide official sources and explain ClearDisk's different role. No affiliation, hands-on competitor test or complete feature replacement is implied.
- Spaced “app cleaner” phrases can also mean a general cleaner. The AppCleaner article connects readers to the cleaner guide, and the cleaner/uninstall guides now link back to the AppCleaner walkthrough. Search Console query data can later show whether that assignment needs refinement.
- “Disk” and “disc,” or different word orders, do not justify near-identical articles. The same practical cleanup guide serves those variants with a clear title and normal prose.
- The generic `mac cleaner app` cluster is served by a buyer-oriented guide that discusses native tools, uninstallers, file scanners and maintenance utilities. It does not claim an independently tested “best” ranking.
- Product copy keeps ClearDisk's macOS 15+ requirement, free local scan and $10 one-time cleanup license. App files, licensing, payments, analytics implementation and the Talivia pause are unchanged.
