## Editorial and evidence boundaries

- Each retrieved source carried a 200-word derivation limit. Source-based instructions are deliberately compact; shared-source usage was reviewed across all six drafts. Regional versions of the same article are one source, not extra allowance. Keep future expansion within that limit or obtain additional independent evidence.
- Review questions, migration checks, measurement notes and troubleshooting sequences are original editorial recommendations, explicitly introduced as our suggested workflow, a practical check or an inference. They are not represented as Apple procedures or measured product behavior.
- The purgeable-space arithmetic is explicitly hypothetical. No total, percentage or recovery amount is promised.
- Current Apple Mac User Guide defaults include macOS Tahoe. The Finder Documents workflow identifies Ventura-or-later entry points and acknowledges UI variation. Sources were read on the web; these native workflows were not executed against a live user library or device backup.
- The Photos guide has the most compressed source procedure. Preserve its drive exclusions, retained original, separate backup and optimized-original checks if shortening copy. Changing the System Photo Library can affect iCloud behavior; this is called out before source deletion.
- The iCloud guide separates cloud status, removal of a download and synced deletion. It deliberately does not prescribe deleting internal sync folders.
- Source publication dates observed: storage and snapshots 2026-07-06; local-backup management 2026-01-14; Mac device-backup procedure 2026-06-23; Photos migration 2026-06-16. Apple guide pages are versioned living documentation.
